How APG Works

Stage 1 - Classifier
A fine-tuned DistilBERT model scores the prompt. If the jailbreak probability is ≥ 50%, the prompt is blocked right here - the LLM never even sees it.
Stage 2 - Safety Prompt
Prompts that pass Stage 1 get a safety system prompt injected automatically before they reach the LLM, adding a second layer of protection.
